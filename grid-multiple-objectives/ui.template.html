Multiple ordered objectives

A heatwave dispatch model must meet electricity demand using renewable generation, gas, and imports. Multi-objective optimization represents policy through three linear objectives: first prevent unserved demand, then reduce carbon, then lower operating cost. Compare lexicographic optimization with the weighted-sum method to see why a single combined score can silently trade away reliability when quantities use different units.
